Mt Eliza Dental Centre's team work together to offer all aspects of dental care. We have been serving the needs of patients across the peninsula since 1993, and we provide a wide range of services to children, adolescents and adults with general to cosmetic dental needs.

## Our promise

At Mt Eliza Dental Centre, we're dedicated to providing comprehensive dental care for the whole family. Our experienced team, including [Dr Raymond and Dr Liza]({=site.url('our-team')}), offers expert general dentistry for all ages, from children to adults.

Beyond routine care, we provide cosmetic dentistry, full mouth rehabilitation, dental implants, and orthodontic treatments like braces and Invisalign, all in one convenient location. This means you can access high-quality specialised care close to home without the hassle of travelling into Melbourne.
