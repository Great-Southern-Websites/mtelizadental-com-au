## Porcelain veneers

If you're looking to transform your smile, porcelain veneers offer a long-lasting, natural-looking solution. At Mt Eliza Dental Centre, we use high-quality, custom-made veneers to improve the shape, colour and alignment of your teeth with minimal tooth preparation.

- **Natural appearance.** Custom-designed to match the shape and shade of your natural teeth.
- **Stain-resistant.** Unlike natural teeth, porcelain resists staining from coffee, tea and wine.
- **Minimally invasive.** Requires only a thin layer of enamel removal compared to other cosmetic treatments.
- **Long-lasting.** With proper care, veneers can last 10 to 15 years or more.
- **Quick improvement.** Corrects discolouration, chips, gaps and minor misalignments in just a couple of visits.

If you're ready for a brighter, more confident smile, book a free, no-obligation consultation to learn more about porcelain veneers.

## Composite veneers

Composite veneers are a versatile and cost-effective way to improve your smile. We use high-quality composite resin to create custom veneers that correct issues such as discolouration, chips, gaps and slight misalignments.

- **Affordable.** Less expensive than porcelain veneers, making them a good option for patients on a budget.
- **Quick and convenient.** Often completed in a single visit, with immediate results.
- **Natural-looking results.** Matched to your natural tooth colour for a pleasing finish.
- **Less tooth preparation.** Requires minimal enamel removal, preserving the structure of your natural teeth.
- **Durable and repairable.** Composite veneers last well with proper care and can be easily repaired if damaged.
- **Improved confidence.** A better-looking smile can give your self-esteem a lift.

Book a consultation to see if composite veneers are right for you.
