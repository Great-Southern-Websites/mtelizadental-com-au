## Injectables

At Mt Eliza Dental Centre, we offer muscle-relaxing injectables to treat TMJ disorders and bruxism (teeth grinding). This non-invasive treatment helps relax overactive jaw muscles, reducing pain, tension and headaches while preventing further tooth wear. Injectables can also improve jaw function and provide long-lasting relief from clenching and grinding.

- **Relieves jaw pain and tension.** Helps relax overactive jaw muscles, reducing discomfort and stiffness.
- **Reduces teeth grinding and clenching.** Lessens the intensity of bruxism, preventing further damage to teeth and gums.
- **Eases headaches and migraines.** Relieves tension headaches caused by muscle strain in the jaw.
- **Improves jaw function.** Improves movement and reduces clicking, locking or tightness in the TMJ.
- **Prevents tooth wear and damage.** Protects teeth from excessive grinding, chipping and fractures.
- **Non-surgical and minimally invasive.** A quick, safe and effective treatment with no downtime.
- **Long-lasting relief.** Results can last several months, reducing the need for frequent treatments.
- **Can slim the jawline.** Softens the appearance of an overdeveloped jaw muscle for a more balanced facial profile.

## Occlusal splint

We offer custom-made splints to treat TMJ disorders and bruxism (teeth grinding). Splints help protect your teeth from damage, reduce jaw pain and tension, and promote proper jaw alignment. By wearing a splint, you can prevent further enamel wear, ease headaches and improve overall jaw function for long-term relief.

- **Protects teeth.** Prevents excessive wear, chipping and fractures caused by grinding and clenching.
- **Reduces jaw pain.** Helps relieve strain on the jaw muscles and joints.
- **Eases headaches and facial pain.** Relieves tension-related discomfort caused by bruxism.
- **Improves jaw alignment.** Supports proper positioning to reduce stress on the TMJ.
- **Prevents further damage.** Helps stop enamel erosion and reduces the risk of long-term dental issues.
- **Better sleep.** Reduces night-time grinding, leading to more restful sleep.
- **Non-invasive.** A simple, effective treatment without the need for medication or surgery.

Book a consultation to see if a custom splint is the right solution for you.
