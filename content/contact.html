Frequently asked questions

Questions we receive from our patients.

I brush my teeth constantly but still have bad breath. What can I do?

Brushing and flossing are the first steps to getting rid of bad breath. They remove the bacteria responsible for creating odorous sulphur compounds, and the food those bacteria feed on. However, bacteria hide not only on and around the teeth but also on the tongue under a layer of mucus, where they are free to create odours.

It is best to brush your tongue daily, or you may want to consider a tongue scraper. Both are very effective at removing this layer from the back of the tongue.

What is cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry combines art and dental science. These procedures aim to create attractive smiles using the latest restorative technologies and materials.

Why is it so important to maintain dental health?

Looking after your teeth and gums as you age is an important part of your general health and wellbeing. Exercise, eating well and regular health checks matter, and so does caring for your teeth and gums. Good dental health will help you lead a long, happy and healthy life.

What are the main benefits of quality dental treatment?

Achieving good dental health can change your life for the better. Some of the benefits dental treatment may provide include:

  • improved confidence and self-esteem
  • improved oral health and general wellbeing
  • improved facial appearance
  • improved comfort
  • improved speech
  • improved dietary habits and nutrition.
What are dental implants?

Dental implants may be used when you have lost a tooth. An implant is a titanium rod that is surgically placed into the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. A crown or bridge is then attached to the rod to restore function and appearance. You can then enjoy chewing, biting and speaking as you did before tooth loss. Read more about implants.

What does full mouth rehabilitation involve?

Full mouth rehabilitation may include a range of cosmetic and restorative dental procedures to restore the health and appearance of your smile. A dental implant or bridge may be needed to replace missing teeth, orthodontics to bring teeth into alignment, or whitening to brighten your smile, among other procedures.

I have crooked teeth. Can you help?

If your teeth are slightly crooked you may benefit from porcelain veneers (often called "instant ortho"). However, in many cases orthodontic treatment may be the best solution for misaligned teeth and bite problems. Orthodontic treatment focuses on straightening teeth and altering bone growth within the mouth. Read about braces and Invisalign.

What does periodontal treatment involve?

Periodontal disease, or periodontitis, is an inflammation of the periodontium (the tissue holding the tooth). If left untreated it can lead to bad breath and eventually tooth loss, and it is the major cause of tooth loss in adults.

Since dental plaque is the main cause of gum disease, treatment focuses on plaque control. This is achieved through improved oral hygiene and cleaning the teeth and their roots to remove plaque and tartar from under the gums. The final objective of any treatment is to clean the gum pocket. Read more about gum disease.

When do I need oral surgery?

You may need minor oral surgery if you have an impacted wisdom tooth or need an extraction. Within our practice we try to save every tooth. Today, tooth extraction is far less painful than ever before. In many cases, a patient who has a tooth extracted will experience little or no discomfort and only minor bleeding.
