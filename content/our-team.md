<h2>Dr Raymond D'Souza</h2>
<p class="role">Principal Dentist / Owner</p>
<p>Dr Raymond D'Souza is a Melbourne University graduate with a wide range of experience in dentistry, having worked for over 15 years in country Victoria, in various practices around Melbourne and in the United Kingdom.</p>
<p>Ray spent over 5 years working in a cosmetic dental practice in Armadale, where in addition to providing the full range of general dental treatments, he specialised in cosmetic dentistry. He gained considerable experience working with veneers, crowns and bridges as well as performing complex dentistry such as full mouth rehabilitations (restoring many worn and damaged teeth), and using implants to replace missing teeth.</p>
<p>Ray also has years of experience in Invisalign (a near-invisible way to straighten teeth without braces), having been part of a practice that was accredited as a Platinum provider of Invisalign.</p>

<h2>Dr Liza Ince</h2>
<p class="role">Principal Dentist / Owner</p>
<p>Dr Liza Ince graduated with honours from the University of Melbourne Dental School in 1999 and has since developed a strong interest in children's dentistry and orthodontics for all ages. She provides treatment using both traditional braces and Invisalign, having completed her orthodontic training with the POS system in 2005.</p>
<p>Dr Ince stays up to date with the latest advances in dental materials, technology and orthodontic care. She has also recently completed training in facial injectables (including Botox®) for treating TMJ disorders.</p>
