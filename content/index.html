Create the smile you want

Mt Eliza Dental Centre has been serving the needs of patients across the peninsula since 1993. We provide a wide range of services to children, adolescents and adults with general to cosmetic dental needs. We provide complex dentistry such as implants, orthodontics and full mouth makeovers.

We use the latest technology to offer you the best results and create the smile you want.

About the practice

Why choose us

We provide comprehensive dental care for the whole family, offering a full range of services in one convenient location.

We keep it simple

We explain your options clearly, with no jargon and no confusion. Enjoy free, no-obligation consultations for cosmetic treatments and Invisalign.

We know you're busy

Life gets busy with work and family, so we offer flexible booking options to fit your schedule, including early morning and evening appointments.

We look after you

We provide high-quality, gentle dental care in a friendly and welcoming environment. Our experienced team puts your comfort first.
