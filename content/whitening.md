A brighter smile can make a big difference to your confidence and appearance. At Mt Eliza Dental Centre, we offer professional teeth whitening, both in-chair and with take-home trays, to safely and effectively lighten the colour of your teeth.

## Why choose professional whitening

- **Safe and dentist-supervised.** Performed under professional guidance to protect your teeth and gums.
- **Fast, effective results.** See noticeable improvements in as little as one session.
- **Treatment options.** Choose in-chair whitening for instant brightness or take-home trays for gradual whitening.
- **Long-lasting, even whitening.** Consistent results that outlast over-the-counter options.
- **Boosts confidence.** A whiter smile can make you look younger and feel more self-assured.

## What to expect

Some stains and discolouration respond better than others. We'll assess your teeth first so you have realistic expectations of what whitening can do for you. Consultations for cosmetic treatments are free, with no obligation.
