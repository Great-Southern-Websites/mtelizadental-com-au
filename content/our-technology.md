## Onsite CBCT

Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) is an advanced imaging technique that provides three-dimensional views of dental structures, including teeth, jawbone and surrounding tissues. It improves the accuracy of many dental procedures by giving detailed insight into each patient's oral anatomy.

### How we use CBCT

- **Dental implant planning.** CBCT allows precise assessment of bone quality and quantity, so implants can be placed in the best position while avoiding vital structures.
- **Endodontics.** It helps identify complex root canal systems and detect hidden problems, improving the success rate of root canal treatment.
- **Oral surgery.** CBCT is used to evaluate impacted teeth, plan extractions and assess jaw abnormalities, leading to more predictable surgical outcomes.
- **Orthodontics.** CBCT gives comprehensive views of the skull and face, helping with diagnosis and treatment planning.

By delivering high-resolution images with relatively low radiation exposure, CBCT has become an essential tool in modern dentistry for both diagnosis and treatment precision.

## iTero intraoral scanner

The iTero scanner offers a fast, precise and comfortable alternative to traditional dental impressions. Using 3D digital imaging, it captures detailed images of your teeth and gums, allowing for highly accurate treatment planning.

It is especially useful for Invisalign, crowns, bridges and other restorative or orthodontic treatments. And there are no more messy impression materials.
