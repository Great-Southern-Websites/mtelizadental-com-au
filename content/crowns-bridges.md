## Crowns

Crowns are a reliable way to restore damaged teeth and improve their appearance. They are often used when a tooth is worn or broken, or when veneers aren't suitable for cosmetic purposes.

A crown fits over the existing tooth, covering and strengthening it and preserving the tooth's structure. Thanks to modern dental ceramics and bonding techniques, crowns can now be made to blend in with your natural teeth for a durable, natural-looking result.

- **Strengthen weakened teeth.** Protects and reinforces teeth that are damaged or compromised.
- **Restore function.** Lets you chew and bite naturally with confidence.
- **Natural appearance.** Natural-looking materials help crowns blend in with your smile.
- **Long-lasting.** Crowns are made to withstand daily use and can last many years with proper care.

If you're dealing with a damaged or weakened tooth, a crown could be the answer. Book a consultation to find out how crowns can restore your smile.

## Bridges

A dental bridge is a durable, natural-looking way to replace missing teeth. It uses crowns placed on the healthy teeth on either side of the gap to support a replacement tooth, bridging the space. Unlike dentures, bridges are fixed in place, giving a stable, permanent result that feels like your natural teeth.

- **Restore function.** Bridges let you chew, speak and smile with confidence, as you would with your natural teeth.
- **Natural look and feel.** Custom-made to match your surrounding teeth for a comfortable result.
- **Permanent and secure.** Unlike removable dentures, bridges are fixed in place for long-lasting comfort and stability.
- **Preserve tooth alignment.** Bridges stop surrounding teeth from shifting into the gap.

If you're missing teeth and want a reliable, comfortable solution, a dental bridge could be right for you. Book a consultation to find out more.
