## Preventative dentistry

At Mt Eliza Dental Centre, we believe regular care is the key to lifelong oral health. That's why we recommend most patients visit us every six months for a check-up and clean, though we tailor this schedule to suit individual needs.

Routine check-ups and professional cleans offer many benefits, including:

- Early detection of issues like cavities, gum disease and oral cancer, helping prevent more serious problems.
- Removal of plaque and tartar build-up, reducing the risk of decay and bad breath.
- Personalised advice on improving your daily oral hygiene for a healthier smile.

### Dental X-rays

Dental X-rays are a valuable tool for detecting problems that may not be visible during a standard examination. They allow us to identify issues early, often before they cause permanent damage, so we can address them while they're still small, easier and more affordable to fix.

We use digital X-ray technology, which significantly reduces exposure time, provides instant results and delivers higher-quality images than traditional film X-rays. If you have any questions about the benefits of dental X-rays, feel free to ask at your next visit.

## Extractions

We understand that having a tooth removed can be daunting, and our team works to make the process as gentle and stress-free as possible. While we always aim to save natural teeth, extractions are sometimes necessary due to severe decay, damage, infection or overcrowding.

Our experienced dentists use modern techniques and local anaesthesia to keep you comfortable throughout the procedure. We also provide clear aftercare instructions to support a smooth recovery and minimise discomfort. If needed, we can discuss tooth replacement options, such as [dental implants]({=site.url('implants')}) or [bridges]({=site.url('crowns-bridges')}), to restore your smile and function.

If you're experiencing persistent tooth pain or think you may need an extraction, contact us to book a consultation. We'll assess your situation and provide care tailored to your needs.

## Fillings

We use high-quality, durable fillings to restore teeth affected by decay, cracks or minor damage. Our goal is to strengthen and protect your teeth while keeping a natural look.

We offer tooth-coloured composite fillings, which blend with your natural teeth and provide both strength and good looks. These fillings bond directly to the tooth, helping to support its structure and prevent further damage.

Getting a filling is a straightforward and comfortable process. After removing any decay, we carefully clean the area before placing and shaping the filling for a precise fit. The result is a strong, long-lasting restoration.

If you have a cavity or suspect you may need a filling, book an appointment with us. Early treatment helps prevent further damage and keeps your teeth healthy for the long run.
