## Invisalign

At Mt Eliza Dental Centre, we offer Invisalign, a discreet and comfortable alternative to traditional braces. Using a series of clear, custom-made aligners, Invisalign gradually straightens your teeth without metal brackets or wires. It suits both teenagers and adults looking for a more subtle way to achieve a straighter smile.

- **Nearly invisible.** The clear aligners are discreet, so most people won't notice you're wearing them.
- **Removable.** Eat, drink, brush and floss with ease by taking out your aligners when needed.
- **Comfortable and gentle.** Smooth plastic aligners cause less irritation to the gums and cheeks than traditional braces.
- **Fewer appointments.** With no wires to adjust, Invisalign typically needs fewer visits to the dentist.
- **Effective for a variety of cases.** Corrects misalignment, crowding, spacing and bite issues for a healthier, straighter smile.

Our team customises every Invisalign treatment plan to suit your needs. Consultations for Invisalign are free, with no obligation. Book a consultation to find out if Invisalign is right for you.

## Conventional orthodontics (braces)

We offer high-quality orthodontic treatment with braces to straighten teeth, correct bite issues and create a healthier, more confident smile. Braces are a reliable and effective solution for patients of all ages, from children and teenagers to adults.

- **Straighten misaligned teeth.** Braces gradually shift teeth into the correct position, improving both appearance and function.
- **Correct bite issues.** They help fix overbites, underbites, crossbites and other alignment problems for better long-term oral health.
- **Prevent future dental problems.** Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of decay, gum disease and excessive wear.
- **Improve speech and chewing.** Braces improve the way teeth fit together, making speaking and eating more comfortable.
- **Boost confidence.** A straight, well-aligned smile can improve self-esteem and overall wellbeing.

Our experienced team tailors each braces treatment plan to your needs, aiming for the best results in the most efficient time frame. Book a consultation to find out how braces can improve your smile and dental health.
