## Benefits of implants

- **Long-lasting.** Implants are a durable, permanent solution for missing teeth.
- **Natural look and feel.** Designed to blend in with your natural teeth for a comfortable, attractive result.
- **Easy to clean.** Implants are maintained with regular brushing and flossing, just like your natural teeth.

## Options for one tooth or many

Whether you're replacing a single tooth, multiple teeth or a full set, dental implants can be tailored to your needs. They are often the preferred choice for people who want a reliable, long-term solution, especially when traditional options fall short on comfort or durability.

We plan implants with our onsite [CBCT imaging]({=site.url('our-technology')}), which shows bone quality and quantity so each implant can be placed in the best position.
